# Rights, attribution and dataset reuse

**Version 1.0 · 19 November 2026**

## Original Double FW material

The repository's [MIT licence](LICENSE) applies to original Double FW Ltd contributions: current methodological guidance, prompt instructions, schemas, templates, source-inventory metadata and newly written fictional examples. Preserve the copyright and permission notices when reusing MIT material. This document adds no commercial-use restriction to MIT.

## Historical compilation and external material

Executive Commitments - RAW Data.txt and its derived readable Markdown edition contain collected third-party wording as well as introductory guidance. Original publication dates, source ownership, licence/permission basis and exact statement boundaries are not established for each extract. The repository-level MIT declaration does not resolve those missing facts or relicense third-party content.

Preserve the compilation as historical source evidence in this repository; this correction does not grant or determine permission for its existing hosting. Exclude it, copied passages and reconstructed statement datasets from the proposed Hugging Face release until each included extract has a documented reuse basis and the relevant approval. A public URL, anonymisation, a schema field set to permitted, or availability in Git history is not proof of permission. Original statements and linked sources retain their own rights.

The current thematic guide was rewritten as Double-FW recommendations and newly authored illustrative candidates. They are labelled as such; they are not attributed quotations, corpus measurements or evidence that any organisation has acted.

## Recording a reuse decision

Record the source URL/title, exact extract and locator, source date/version, rightsholder or unknown, requested use, supporting licence/permission or other reviewed basis, actual reviewer/decision reference and decision date. If any material point is unresolved, keep reuse_status as not_assessed or permission_required and do not redistribute the extract through the proposed dataset. Do not invent missing provenance or infer an organisation from brand clues.

## Release selection

[evidence/dataset-selection.json](evidence/dataset-selection.json) is the explicit original-material selection enforced by the installed exporter. Raw material in both text and Markdown forms, its derived physical-segment inventory, operational evidence, temporary files and historical revisions are excluded. The exporter enforces the selection and carries the MIT notice, source path/revision, evidence limitations and fictional-example labels. This selection is documentation/configuration, not proof that export or upload has occurred. Review it when files or rights change.
