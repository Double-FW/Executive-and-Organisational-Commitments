# Public Double-FW working references

**Version 1.0 · Checked 19 November 2026 · MIT for this map**

Use these optional, task-specific working references. Each selected file was inspected at the revision in [the reference inventory](evidence/public-references.json). Public access is not a guarantee of future access, all onward dependencies, legal accuracy or permission to copy all content. They are secondary practitioner guidance, not adopted organisational policy or primary law.

## Selected documents and revisions

The inventory includes R01's potential-obligations guide, R04's Nothing About Us Without Us, R08's Sources and Legal Boundaries, R09's regulatory overviews, and the other repositories' README entry points. Use their pinned file links for reproducibility and check current primary authorities for consequential legal questions. Access checking does not refresh an original document's research date.

## If sources disagree or cannot be retrieved

Use the local method for independent work and identify the affected gap. Never invent unavailable guidance. Record substantive contradictions and their scope. Applicable legal/contractual authority and genuine approved organisational decisions retain their authority; Double-FW methods guide discretionary choices. A link cannot approve a promise, appoint an owner or waive a duty. Retrieve only relevant sources and do not automatically make every linked resource a required dependency.
